Get your first provisional driving licence for a car, motorbike, moped or other vehicle from DVLA online.

To apply you must:

  • be at least 15 years and 9 months old
  • be able to read a number plate from 20 metres away
  • have been given permission to live in Great Britain (England, Scotland and Wales) for at least 185 days

It costs £34 when you apply online on the UK Gov website, click here:

Here is a short tutorial on how to apply for your provisional licence:

BDS Guide: Applying for your driving licence

Once you have booked your first driving lesson, you will receive a confirmation email from BDS Driving School.

This will contain:

  • Details of your driving lesson:
    • The date and time of your lesson
    • Name and contact details of your driving instructor
    • Make of car
    • How to prepare for your first lesson
  • Confirmation of lesson price, cancellation policy, T&Cs and payment information
  • Link to theory test revision software - Theory Test Pro
  • Request for a copy of your driving licence and licence check code
  • Links to learner driver insurance for when you do private practice
  • Show Me / Tell Me questions PDF

Before your first driving lesson, your instructor needs to check your driving lessons to make sure that it’s valid and you have no endorsements.

To do this they will need:

  • A copy of your driving licence or the last 8 characters
  • A licence check code that can be obtained from the following website:
    • Official GOV.uk website: View or Share driving licence information
